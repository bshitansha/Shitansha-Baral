Welcome to Travel Nepal

Explore the beauty of Nepal with our guided tours and unforgettable experiences. We provide amazing adventures to mountains, lakes, heritage sites and valleys for all travelers.
At Travel Nepal, we members are passionate, dedicated to sharing the beauty and cultural richness of Nepal with travelers from around the world. With years of experience as trekking guides for major French trekking agencies and extensive training in tourism, we bring a wealth of knowledge and expertise to ensure your adventure is unforgettable. Travel Nepal specializes in a wide range of tours that cater to both seasoned trekkers and casual travelers. Whether you're seeking a challenging trek in the Himalayas or a cultural tour of Nepal's vibrant cities, we have something for everyone.
We believe in providing personalized and memorable experiences. Whether you’re traveling in a large group or seeking a more intimate adventure, we tailor our tours to meet your needs. Our deep knowledge of the terrain and local culture, combined with our commitment to exceptional service, ensures that your journey with us will be both safe and inspiring. Join us at Travel Nepal, and let us guide you through the wonders of Nepal, where every path leads to a new discovery.
